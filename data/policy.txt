ENTERPRISE DATA ACCESS POLICY
==============================

1. GENERAL POLICY
All employees must comply with data access rules defined by their role.
Unauthorized access to restricted data is strictly prohibited.

2. HR DEPARTMENT
HR staff can access employee records including names, departments, and roles.
HR cannot access salary data or engineering audit logs.

3. FINANCE DEPARTMENT
Finance staff can access employee salary data and financial records.
Finance cannot access HR personal notes or engineering logs.

4. ENGINEERING DEPARTMENT
Engineers can access system audit logs and technical reports.
Engineers cannot access salary or personal employee data.

5. ADMIN
Admin users have full access to all data sources and systems.
All admin actions are logged and reviewed weekly.

6. COMPLIANCE
All data access is logged automatically.
Violations are reported to the security team immediately.
Data retention period is 7 years for financial records.
